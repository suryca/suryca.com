Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Suryca Software Inc. ("Suryca", "we") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website and products, including Fizgot, ExportAIChat, and Suryca Agents.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — such as your name, email, and the content you create or import — and limited technical data like device, browser, and usage events that help us keep products reliable and secure.

2. How we use information

We use your information to provide and improve our products, communicate with you, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Your content

Content you create or import stays yours. We process it only to deliver the product you're using — for example, generating output in Fizgot or exporting a conversation in ExportAIChat — and according to your settings.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate (under contract), when required by law, or to protect rights and safety. Any provider acting on our behalf is bound to protect your information.

5. Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it. We protect it with encryption in transit and at rest and least-privilege access. See our Security page for details.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data. To exercise them, email us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes & contact

We'll update this policy as our products evolve and note the date above. Questions? Email privacy@suryca.com.
